Fendas doTempo

Rifts of Time

Physical melee combat in virtual reality, carving a path through the eras of history.

Platform Meta Quest 2 & 3 Co-op up to 4 players Engine Godot 4 + OpenXR
Playable right now

Every hit comes from your arm.

No attack button. Your weapon follows your hand, and the damage comes from how fast and how well you swing.

physical combat

Swing hard, hit hard

Blade speed sets the damage. Blades cut, axes and clubs crush, and the spear pierces when you thrust along the shaft.

defense

Parry and block

Shield strapped to your forearm, parries with your sword, and enemy posture that cracks when you hold a solid guard.

arsenal

Sword, axe, spear, bow

Round shield, a bow with a quiver on your back, laser pistols for the eras of the future. Anything goes in either hand and stows in a sheath or on your back.

spoken magic

Say the spell out loud

Grip your amulet or grimoire and speak. 10 spells from the 1st to the 5th circle, from heal and fireball all the way to meteor and time stop. The grimoire shows every spell and exactly what to say.

rooms & portals

Clear the room, open the portal

Every dungeon is a chain of rooms packed with enemies that fan out around you. Take them all down and the portal appears; the boss waits in the final room.

characters

Warrior, Mage or Hunter

Each class starts with different attributes. Every 3 levels you earn an attribute point, and the mage learns or empowers a spell every 2 levels.

co-op

Four friends, one fight

Up to 4 players take on the same enemies over any network, with built-in voice chat. Attacks land in sync for everyone, so every parry and block counts.

inventory

Potions on your belt, weapons on Y

Reach for your belt, grab a potion and raise it to your mouth to drink. The Y button opens your inventory: stow one weapon and draw another mid-fight. Back in your cabin, a chest holds the rest.

first boss

Rostam, hero of Persia

Three phases: the mace that splits the ground, the lasso and Rakhsh the sand horse, then a rain of arrows. He talks to you, and his trophy ends up on your cabin shelf.

appearance

Your face in the mirror

Your cabin has a mirror: pick body, skin tone, hair, beard and hair color. In co-op your friends see your whole body, weapons stowed right on you.

co-op

No one gets left behind

Down? You have 30 seconds. A friend lays both hands on you or casts Heal and you are back in the fight. Playing solo, the Sand of Chronos picks you up once per room.

weapons by era

Shamshir, gladius, rapier

Each era hides its own weapons in golden chests: Persian shamshir and tabarzin, Roman gladius and scutum, mace, war hammer, rapier and flintlock pistol.

grimoire

Quick guide on X

Press X and the grimoire opens in front of you: one chapter per circle, every spell command in big letters. Heal and Shield go to whichever friend you aim at.

Time map

Four dungeons, four bosses.

The game launches with four dungeons, each set in its own era and each telling part of the story. Fight your way through room by room; the era's boss waits in the last one.

Persian Empire · c. 500 BC

Fortress Courtyard

An empire at war as night falls. Smoke on the horizon, braziers ablaze and soldiers guarding the palace gate.

Boss: Rostam, hero of the Shahnameh

Wallachia · 1462

The Forgotten Dungeon

Stairs, cells, the water wheel and the Dragon Knight's armory. In the throne room, under the red moon, the prince is waiting.

Boss: Vlad III

Rome · 192 AD

The Emperor's Arena

Tunnels, gladiator cells, the beast lift and a packed Colosseum. Retiarius, murmillo, a lion and the champion Narcissus.

Boss: Commodus, the gladiator emperor

Milan · 1495

The Machine Workshop

The Renaissance invaded by the future: automatons, drones, the giant crossbow and a bridge over a chasm of energy.

Boss: Leonardo da Vinci and the Colossus

Who you will face

Warriors from across the ages.

Every enemy telegraphs its strike, keeps its guard up and falls when its posture breaks.

Co-op

The Time Ship.

This is where your party meets up when you jump in.

Whoever creates the room becomes the host and runs the match. Rooms can be public or private, with a six-letter code to bring your friends in. Four players fit: the host plus three.

The story

Chronos chose you.

The thread of history has snapped, tearing rifts into its defining moments. Chronos, guardian of time, cannot enter them, so he chose four friends to cross the eras and seal every rift. The opening is narrated in English or Portuguese the first time you play.

Download the APK for Quest · 176 MB
  1. Turn on developer mode for your Quest in the Meta Horizon app on your phone.
  2. Install the APK with SideQuest (or adb install fendas_do_tempo.apk) while the Quest is plugged into your computer.
  3. On the Quest, open your library, filter by Unknown Sources and launch Fendas do Tempo.

You only install the APK once: when a new version ships, the game downloads the update by itself on launch. Voice magic works on the Quest (speech recognition over the internet) and multiplayer works on any network. Coming soon to the SideQuest store.

comfort

Intense

Fantasy combat against historical warriors, monsters and machines (no blood). Expect magic flashes and lightning, smooth thumbstick locomotion, jumps and falls. Play standing. Not recommended if you get motion sick in VR.

known issues

Early access

Some enemies look too dark in low-light rooms, and some bosses still use placeholder models. Spoken magic needs a quiet room and clearly spoken English words. Quest 2 performance is still being tuned. Screenshots on this site come from the PC development build.

privacy

No ads, no tracking

The microphone is used only for spoken spells and in-room voice chat; your voice is never recorded. Read the privacy policy and the credits, or report a problem.
