Privacy Policy

Fendas do Tempo (com.devalexandre.fendasdotempo) — last updated 2026-11-08.

Fendas do Tempo is a free VR game in early access, made by an independent developer. This page explains what the game collects and why. Short version: no ads, no analytics, no selling of data, and voice is never recorded.

What we collect

  • Player name and an anonymous player ID. When you go online, the game sends the character name you typed and a random device identifier. The server keeps only a one-way hash of that identifier as your player ID, plus the name and the date of your last login. This lets you create and join rooms and keep your private rooms.
  • Room information. While you host a room, the server stores the room name, your public and local IP address (so friends can connect) and the number of players. Rooms are deleted when you stop hosting.
  • Microphone audio. The game asks for microphone permission for two features:
    • Spoken spells: while you hold your amulet or grimoire, the audio is streamed to our speech server, which only matches it against the list of spell words. The audio is processed in memory and is not stored.
    • Voice chat: your voice is sent through our relay to the other players in your room, in real time. It is not recorded.
  • Performance metrics and diagnostics. In test builds (currently 0.8.5) they are on by default to find a rendering problem; you can turn them off in the game menu (Vídeo → "Enviar métricas de desempenho"). Besides frame rates, test builds send the game engine's error messages, headset/GPU model and VR session state. When on, "Enviar métricas de desempenho" in the game menu, the game sends frame-rate numbers every 30 seconds: frames per second, frame times, draw calls, which room you are in, quality setting, headset model and game version, tied to your anonymous player ID. We use them only to make the game run smoothly and delete them regularly. Turn the option off to stop.
  • On your headset only: your characters, inventory and settings are saved locally on the device.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs (only the optional performance metrics above), no sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • We don't ask for your real name, email, location or payment data.

Where it runs

The login/room server runs on Google Cloud (App Engine) with a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon; the game relay and speech server run on Google Cloud Run. Content updates are downloaded from GitHub Releases.

Children

The game follows the Meta Quest age requirements and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can deny the microphone permission: spoken spells and voice chat stop working, everything else still works.
  • To delete your player record, open an issue or contact us (below) with your character name; we delete it.

Contact

Report problems or privacy requests at github.com/devalexandre/fendas-do-tempo/issues.
